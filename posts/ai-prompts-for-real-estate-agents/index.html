The key is having prompts that are specific, detailed, and tailored to real estate. Generic prompts like “write me a listing description” give you generic results. The prompts below are engineered to give you professional-grade output every time.

Best AI Prompts for Real Estate Listing Descriptions#

Your listing description is often the first impression a buyer gets. A great description doesn’t just list features — it sells a lifestyle.

The Luxury Listing Prompt#

“Write a luxury real estate listing description for a [bedrooms]-bedroom, [bathrooms]-bathroom home at [address] in [neighborhood]. The home is [square footage] sq ft, built in [year], and priced at $[price]. Key features include: [list features]. Write in an aspirational, lifestyle-focused tone that appeals to affluent buyers. Highlight the neighborhood, nearby amenities, and what makes this property unique. Keep it under 300 words.”

This prompt works because it forces the AI to focus on lifestyle over specs. Luxury buyers don’t care about how many outlets are in the kitchen — they care about entertaining guests on the wraparound terrace with city views.

The First-Time Buyer Prompt#

“Write a warm, approachable listing description for a [bedrooms]-bedroom starter home at [address] priced at $[price]. Target audience is first-time homebuyers in their late 20s to mid 30s. Emphasize value, potential, and the benefits of homeownership vs. renting. Mention: [key features]. Keep the tone encouraging and exciting — this is someone’s first home. Under 250 words.”

The Investment Property Prompt#

“Write a listing description for an investment property at [address]. Include: [property details]. Target audience is real estate investors. Focus on ROI potential, rental income estimates, cap rate, neighborhood growth trends, and cash flow potential. Be data-driven and persuasive. Under 250 words.”

AI Prompts for Real Estate Marketing Emails#

Email is still one of the highest-ROI marketing channels in real estate. These prompts help you write emails that actually get opened and clicked.

The New Listing Announcement#

“Write a marketing email announcing a new listing at [address] priced at $[price]. Include property highlights: [features]. The email should create urgency, include a clear call-to-action to schedule a showing, and feel personal — not like a mass blast. Subject line options included. Under 200 words.”

The Market Update Email#

“Write a monthly real estate market update email for [city/neighborhood]. Include talking points about: median home prices, inventory levels, days on market, and interest rate trends. Position me as the local market expert. Include a soft CTA to reach out for a free home valuation. Professional but conversational tone.”

The Follow-Up Sequence#

“Write a 3-email follow-up sequence for a lead who attended an open house but hasn’t scheduled a private showing. Email 1 (send day after): Thank them, highlight what they saw, include new photos. Email 2 (send day 4): Share a market insight about the neighborhood, create subtle urgency. Email 3 (send day 7): Final follow-up with a new angle — maybe a price comparison or new comparable listing. Each email under 150 words.”

AI Prompts for Social Media Content#

Social media is where buyers and sellers discover agents. These prompts help you create scroll-stopping content without spending hours on it.

The Instagram Carousel Prompt#

“Create a 7-slide Instagram carousel about [topic, e.g., ‘7 Things First-Time Buyers Need to Know in 2026’]. For each slide, provide: a headline (under 8 words), 2-3 bullet points, and a brief explanation. Make it educational, visually structured, and include a call-to-action on the final slide. Target audience: [first-time buyers / sellers / investors].”

The Video Script Prompt#

“Write a 60-second video script for a real estate agent’s Instagram Reel or TikTok about [topic]. Hook the viewer in the first 3 seconds. Keep it conversational, high-energy, and end with a CTA to follow or DM for more info. Include text overlay suggestions.”

The Monthly Content Calendar#

“Create a 30-day social media content calendar for a real estate agent in [market]. Include a mix of: listing posts, market updates, buyer tips, seller tips, personal brand content, neighborhood spotlights, and engagement posts. For each day, provide: the content type, a caption idea, and suggested hashtags. Focus on [target audience].”

AI Prompts for Lead Generation#

These prompts help you attract and convert more leads.

The Neighborhood Guide#

“Write a comprehensive neighborhood guide for [neighborhood] in [city]. Include: overview, housing market snapshot, schools, restaurants, parks, commute times, lifestyle vibe, and who this neighborhood is best for. Write it as an SEO blog post that a real estate agent could publish on their website. Under 1,000 words. Include a CTA to contact me for available listings in the area.”

The Buyer Consultation Script#

“Create a buyer consultation script for a real estate agent meeting a new client for the first time. Cover: introduction, understanding their needs, explaining the buying process, setting expectations, discussing financing, and next steps. Make it conversational, not robotic. Include questions to ask and talking points for each section.”

The Seller CMA Presentation#

“Create an outline for a seller CMA (Comparative Market Analysis) presentation. Include sections for: market overview, comparable sales analysis, pricing strategy recommendation, marketing plan overview, and agent value proposition. For each section, provide talking points and data points to include.”

How to Get the Most Out of AI Prompts#

Having great prompts is step one. Here’s how to maximize your results:

1. Be Specific With Details The more context you give AI, the better the output. Don’t just say “write a listing description.” Include the address, features, target buyer, price point, and tone you want.

2. Iterate and Refine Your first output won’t always be perfect. Use follow-up prompts like “make it more urgent,” “add more emotional language,” or “shorten this by 30%.”

3. Add Your Voice AI gives you a foundation. Always add your personal touch — local knowledge, inside jokes about the neighborhood, your unique perspective. That’s what sets you apart.

4. Build a Prompt Library Save your best-performing prompts in a document you can reference anytime. Over time, you’ll have a custom toolkit that makes content creation almost instant.
