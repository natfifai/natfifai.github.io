Selling digital products is one of the fastest ways to build passive income in 2026. No inventory. No shipping. No overhead. You create it once and sell it forever.

But here’s where most people get stuck: they don’t know what to sell.

This guide breaks down the 12 best digital products to sell online right now — with real profit margins, platforms to sell on, and how AI tools can help you create products 10x faster than doing it from scratch.

Why Digital Products Are the Best Side Hustle in 2026#

Before we dive in, let’s talk numbers.

  • Profit margins: 85-95% (compared to 30-50% for physical products)
  • Startup cost: $0-$50
  • Time to first sale: Days, not months
  • Scalability: Unlimited — selling to 1 person costs the same as selling to 10,000

The digital product market hit $331 billion in 2025 and is projected to keep growing at 10%+ annually. The barrier to entry has never been lower, especially with AI tools that can help you create products in hours instead of weeks.

Now let’s get into the best products to sell.

1. AI Prompt Packs#

Profit margin: 95% | Difficulty: Low | Income potential: $500-$5,000/month

This is the hottest digital product category right now, and for good reason. Millions of people use ChatGPT, Claude, and other AI tools daily — but most of them don’t know how to write effective prompts.

That’s where prompt packs come in. You create collections of tested, proven prompts for specific niches and sell them as ready-to-use tools.

Popular niches for prompt packs:

  • Small business owners who want AI help with marketing, operations, and customer service
  • Content creators who need prompts for scripts, captions, and content calendars
  • Real estate agents using AI for listings, follow-ups, and market analysis
  • Freelancers automating client work with AI

The key is niche specificity. A generic “100 ChatGPT Prompts” pack won’t sell. But “47 AI Prompts That Help Real Estate Agents Close More Deals” absolutely will.

👉 If you want to see what a quality prompt pack looks like, check out the AI Prompt Pack for Small Business ($19) — it’s built with tested prompts that actually produce usable results, not generic filler.

For content creators specifically, the AI Prompt Pack for Content Creators ($19) covers everything from viral hooks to email sequences.

2. Spreadsheet Templates#

Profit margin: 95% | Difficulty: Low-Medium | Income potential: $300-$3,000/month

People will pay good money to skip the setup work. Budget trackers, project planners, client CRMs, invoice generators — if it saves someone 5+ hours of building from scratch, it’s worth $10-$30.

Best-selling spreadsheet templates:

  • Business expense trackers
  • Freelancer income and tax trackers
  • Content calendars
  • Inventory management sheets
  • Sales pipeline trackers

The Freelancer Income Tracker ($15) is a great example — freelancers need to track income across multiple clients, categorize expenses for taxes, and see their profit margins at a glance. Most of them are doing this on napkins or not at all.

Pro tip: Use Google Sheets for your templates (not Excel). More people have access, it auto-syncs, and you can create locked templates that look professional.

3. Online Courses#

Profit margin: 90% | Difficulty: Medium-High | Income potential: $1,000-$50,000/month

Online courses remain one of the highest-earning digital product categories. The difference in 2026 is that you don’t need a film crew or a $10,000 production setup. Your phone, a good microphone, and a screen recorder are enough.

What sells:

  • Skill-based courses (Excel, Photoshop, coding basics)
  • Business courses (how to start freelancing, launch a product)
  • AI courses (how to use ChatGPT for your job, prompt engineering)

If you’re serious about creating courses, invest in a solid USB microphone. The Elgato Wave:3 is studio-quality without the studio price, and it makes a massive difference in how professional your content sounds.

4. Notion Templates#

Profit margin: 95% | Difficulty: Low | Income potential: $200-$5,000/month

Notion has exploded in popularity, and its marketplace of templates is a goldmine. People want pre-built systems for:

  • Project management
  • Habit tracking
  • Content planning
  • CRM and client management
  • Personal finance dashboards

The beauty of Notion templates is they cost nothing to create and people pay $5-$30 for them regularly. Some top creators on Gumroad are doing $10K+/month selling Notion templates alone.

5. Ebooks and Guides#

Profit margin: 95% | Difficulty: Medium | Income potential: $200-$5,000/month

Ebooks aren’t dead — they’ve evolved. The ones that sell in 2026 are hyper-specific, actionable guides that solve a single problem.

Not “How to Make Money Online” (too broad). Instead:

  • “The Freelance Writer’s Guide to Landing $500+ Blog Clients”
  • “Instagram Reels Strategy: A 30-Day Playbook for Service Businesses”
  • “The Remote Worker’s Tax Guide for 2026”

Write your ebook in Google Docs or Notion, export as PDF, and sell on Gumroad. Total cost: $0.

For writing and research, a good tablet makes the process way more portable. The Kindle Scribe lets you read, annotate, and write in one device — perfect if you’re building a content business.

6. Calculators and Interactive Tools#

Profit margin: 95% | Difficulty: Medium | Income potential: $200-$2,000/month

Interactive spreadsheets that calculate something specific are surprisingly profitable. Think:

  • Side hustle profitability calculators
  • Freelance rate calculators
  • ROI calculators for ad spend
  • Savings goal trackers
  • Rental property analyzers

The Side Hustle Calculator ($12) is exactly this — you plug in your numbers and it tells you whether your side hustle idea is actually worth pursuing or if you’re better off spending that time elsewhere.

People love tools that give them personalized answers. That’s why calculators convert so well.

7. Stock Photos and Graphics Packs#

Profit margin: 90% | Difficulty: Medium | Income potential: $100-$3,000/month

With Canva and AI image generators, creating branded graphic packs is easier than ever. What sells:

  • Social media template packs (Instagram stories, LinkedIn carousels)
  • Brand kits with color palettes, font pairings, and logo templates
  • Stock photo bundles for specific niches (wellness, tech, food)
  • Icon and illustration sets

Sell these on Gumroad, Etsy, or Creative Market. Small businesses will pay $15-$50 for a pack that makes their social media look professional without hiring a designer.

8. Email Templates and Swipe Files#

Profit margin: 95% | Difficulty: Low | Income potential: $200-$2,000/month

Every business needs to send emails. Most business owners are terrible at it. Sell them the words:

  • Cold outreach templates for freelancers
  • Follow-up email sequences for sales teams
  • Welcome email series for new customers
  • Launch email templates for product releases
  • Client onboarding email sequences

Package 10-20 templates together, price at $19-$39, and market to the specific audience. Freelancers, real estate agents, and coaches are great buyers for email template packs.

9. Printables#

Profit margin: 95% | Difficulty: Low | Income potential: $100-$5,000/month

Printables have been a steady seller for years and they’re not slowing down. The best part: you can create them in Canva for free.

Top-selling printable categories:

  • Planners and journals
  • Wall art and quotes
  • Checklists and trackers
  • Educational worksheets
  • Wedding and event planning kits

Etsy is the dominant marketplace for printables, but Gumroad works too. Some printable sellers are doing $50K+/year with nothing but Canva and SEO.

10. Audio Products (Meditation, Music, Sound Effects)#

Profit margin: 90% | Difficulty: Medium | Income potential: $100-$2,000/month

If you have any audio skills — or are willing to learn — this is an underserved market:

  • Guided meditation recordings
  • Ambient sound packs (for content creators)
  • Podcast intro/outro music
  • Sound effects for video editors
  • ASMR and focus audio

A decent microphone is essential here. The Audio-Technica AT2020 is the industry standard for beginners — professional quality at a fraction of the price of studio mics.

11. Website Templates and Themes#

Profit margin: 90% | Difficulty: Medium-High | Income potential: $500-$10,000/month

If you know HTML/CSS or can build in Webflow, Framer, or WordPress, selling templates is incredibly lucrative. Businesses pay $29-$199 for a single website template.

Hot niches:

  • Landing pages for SaaS companies
  • Portfolio sites for freelancers
  • Restaurant and local business templates
  • E-commerce themes for Shopify

Even without coding skills, you can create Carrd or Canva website templates and sell them for $10-$30.

12. Coaching and Consulting Frameworks#

Profit margin: 95% | Difficulty: Medium | Income potential: $500-$5,000/month

Package your expertise into frameworks, playbooks, and SOPs that other professionals can license and use:

  • Sales call scripts and objection-handling guides
  • Client onboarding systems
  • Social media strategy playbooks
  • Hiring and interview frameworks

These typically sell at higher price points ($39-$99+) because they’re saving businesses real operational time.

How to Get Started Today#

You don’t need all 12. Pick one product type that matches your skills, and get it live within a week. Here’s the formula:

  1. Choose your niche — Who are you selling to? Be specific.
  2. Create the product — Use AI tools to speed up creation. A good prompt pack like the AI Prompt Pack for Small Business can help you brainstorm, outline, and draft your product 5x faster.
  3. Set up on Gumroad — Free to start, they take a small cut per sale.
  4. Drive traffic — SEO blog content, Pinterest, Twitter/X, and short-form video.
  5. Iterate — Your first product won’t be perfect. Ship it, get feedback, improve.

The people making real money aren’t waiting for the perfect product. They’re shipping, testing, and iterating.
