# Fifer Digital — Affiliate Blog Business Plan

## Overview
Automated SEO content site that drives traffic through Google search and monetizes with:
1. Affiliate links (Amazon Associates, software tools)
2. Direct links to our Gumroad digital products
3. Future: display ads (once traffic hits 10K+ sessions/month)

## Platform
- **Site:** Hugo static site hosted on GitHub Pages (FREE)
- **URL:** fiferdigital.github.io (can add custom domain later)
- **Theme:** PaperMod (clean, fast, SEO-optimized)
- **Publishing:** Pimp writes articles, git pushes, auto-deploys

## Content Strategy

### Target Keywords (Buyer Intent)
These are searches where people are ready to spend money:

**AI Prompts Niche:**
- "best ai prompts for small business"
- "chatgpt prompts for real estate agents"
- "ai prompts for content creators"
- "best chatgpt prompts for marketing"
- "how to use ai for business"

**Freelance/Business Niche:**
- "freelancer expense tracker spreadsheet"
- "how to track freelance income"
- "freelancer tax deductions 2026"
- "best spreadsheet for freelancers"

**Side Hustle Niche:**
- "is my side hustle profitable"
- "side hustle profit calculator"
- "best side hustles 2026"
- "how to start a digital product business"

### Content Types
1. **Product-Led Articles** (drive Gumroad sales)
   - "The 10 Best AI Prompts for [audience]" → links to our prompt packs
   - "How to Track Freelance Income" → links to our tracker
   - "Is Your Side Hustle Profitable?" → links to our calculator

2. **Affiliate Review Articles** (drive affiliate commissions)
   - "Best AI Tools for Small Business 2026"
   - "Best Invoicing Software for Freelancers"
   - "Best Platforms to Sell Digital Products"

3. **Educational/SEO Articles** (drive organic traffic)
   - "How to Use ChatGPT for [specific use case]"
   - "Freelancer Tax Guide 2026"
   - "How to Price Digital Products"

### Publishing Schedule
- **Target:** 3-5 articles per week (automated via Pimp)
- **Length:** 1,500-3,000 words each
- **Goal:** 50 articles in first 2 months → organic traffic starts compounding

## Revenue Projections

### Month 1-2: Foundation
- 20-50 articles published
- Minimal traffic (Google indexing)
- Revenue: $0-50

### Month 3-4: Traction
- 50-100 articles live
- Organic traffic begins (~1,000-5,000 sessions/month)
- Revenue: $100-500/month (Gumroad sales + affiliate)

### Month 6+: Growth
- 150+ articles
- 10,000+ sessions/month
- Revenue: $500-2,000/month
- Add display ads (Mediavine/Ezoic) at this point

### Month 12+: Scale
- 300+ articles
- 30,000+ sessions/month
- Revenue: $2,000-5,000+/month
